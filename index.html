About Elio

Elio is a safe, anonymous space where you can say the things you can't say anywhere else. No accounts. No history. No judgment.

On the other side of every conversation is a real human listening, present, and genuinely there for you.

Every session ends and disappears. Nothing is stored. Nothing follows you.

Rules

What's allowed

  • Sharing anything on your mind freely and honestly
  • Taking your time, there's no rush here
  • Ending the session whenever you need to

What's not allowed

  • Harassment or abuse toward the listener
  • Sharing illegal content or intentions
  • Attempting to identify or locate the listener
  • Using Elio for anything other than honest conversation

Violations

Sessions involving rule violations will be ended immediately.

Privacy Policy

Elio is built on anonymity. Here is exactly what we do and don't store.

What we never store

  • Your messages — they exist only during the session
  • Your name — even if you provide one, it's forgotten after the session
  • Your identity — no account, no profile, no trace

What we do store

  • Session duration and payment amount — for our own records only
  • Your rating and feedback — anonymously, with no link to you

Your browser

If you choose to save your payment details for faster sessions, a secure token is stored only in your browser's local storage. Elio never sees your card information. Clearing your browser data removes this token completely.

Refund Policy

Sessions are priced at $5 for 30 minutes. Extensions are $5 each.

No refunds after minute 5

Once 5 minutes of a session have passed, the session is considered delivered and no refund will be issued.

Genuine emergencies

Refunds for genuine emergencies are issued at Elio's sole discretion. If something urgent came up before minute 5, reach out via the complaints section.

Declined sessions

If you decline payment when prompted, the session ends immediately. No charge is made for the conversation prior to the payment prompt.
