About Elio

Elio is a safe, anonymous space where you can say the things you can't say anywhere else. No accounts. No history. No judgment.

On the other side of every conversation is a real human listening, present, and genuinely there for you.

We don't know who you are and we never will. Every conversation is treated as completely new. Nothing follows you.

Every conversation ends and disappears. Nothing is stored. Nothing follows you.

Rules

What's allowed

  • Sharing anything on your mind freely and honestly
  • Taking your time, there's no rush here
  • Ending the conversation whenever you need to

What's not allowed

  • Harassment or abuse toward the listener
  • Sharing illegal content or intentions
  • Attempting to identify or locate the listener
  • Using Elio for anything other than honest conversation

Violations

Conversations involving rule violations will be ended immediately.

Privacy Policy

Elio is built on anonymity. Here is exactly what we do and don't store.

What we never store

  • Your messages only exists during the conversation.
  • Your identity. There is no account, no profile, no trace
  • Your payment card details. We never see them; they go directly to our payment processor

What we do store

  • Conversation duration and payment amount - for our own records only
  • Your rating and feedback - anonymously, with no link to you

Anonymity is absolute

We don't know who you are. Every time you book a conversation, you are a brand new person to us. There is no way to connect one conversation to another.

Refund Policy

Each conversation is priced at $5 for 30 minutes.

No refunds after payment

Once you’ve paid for a conversation, it is considered delivered and non‑refundable. The 5‑minute free trial gives you enough time to decide if you want to continue.

Declining of Payment

If you decline payment when prompted, the conversation ends immediately. No charge is made for the conversation prior to the payment prompt.
